Interpretation

An RMSE near 0.79 means predictions are typically off by about 0.79 expected goals per team-match on brand-new 2025 data. Since the target standard deviation is about 0.82, the model is learning some repeatable structure but still leaves most match-level xG variation unexplained.

The model family is reasonable for the available design matrix: ridge handles the sparse indicator structure, while ExtraTrees can capture nonlinear team/opponent/travel effects. The main ceiling appears to come from feature availability. The strongest soccer signal features in the raw table, especially shots and shots on target, are not part of the frozen design matrix used by run.py.
